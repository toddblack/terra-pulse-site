*Last updated: 28 October 2026*

Terra Pulse for Android is an app for exploring earthquakes alongside space
weather and the Earth's magnetic field, on a 3D globe. It is built by Todd
Black. This policy covers the Android app.

## The short version

- **No account, no sign-in, no tracking.** The app has no analytics, no
  telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting. Nothing about you or how
  you use the app is sent to the developer.
- **It never asks for your location**, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  The only permissions it uses are internet access (to fetch public science
  data) and vibration (for its in-app earthquake alerts).
- **It does fetch public science data from the internet.** Like any request
  your phone makes, those reach the servers listed below, which see your IP
  address.

## What the app stores

Nothing of yours. The app keeps no settings, history or location. Your layer
choices last only while the app is open. Android's web view may cache
downloaded map images and data files on the phone, as a browser does;
clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes them.

Earthquake alerts are worked out on the phone, from the same public
earthquake feed the globe shows, and only while the app is open.

## What the app sends over the internet

Terra Pulse downloads public scientific data directly from the organisations
that publish it. Each request goes straight from your phone to that
organisation's server, never through a server run by the developer. As with
any web request, that server can see your **IP address** and the app's name
and version. Their own privacy policies govern what they do with it.

- **Earthquakes:** the U.S. Geological Survey (earthquake.usgs.gov).
- **Space weather** (aurora, ionosphere, solar wind, the Dst index): NOAA's
  Space Weather Prediction Center (services.swpc.noaa.gov).
- **The Kp index:** GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences (kp.gfz.de).
- **Map imagery:** OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org).

Links you tap (a quake's USGS page, OpenStreetMap's copyright page, Cesium's
site) open in your browser, under that site's own policy.

Google Play, which installs and updates the app, handles data under
Google's own privacy policy.

## Children

Terra Pulse is a general-audience science app. It does not knowingly collect
any information from anyone, children included.
